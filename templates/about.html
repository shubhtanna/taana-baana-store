Our weavers

Taana Baana means warp and weft: the two sets of threads that make every handwoven cloth. We started in Jaipur in 2018 with three block printers from Bagru, and now work with 23 weaving and printing families across seven states.

We pay for every piece upfront, at prices the makers set, and we design around what each cluster does best: ikat in Pochampally, silk-cotton in Maheshwar, tant in Shantipur, chikankari in Lucknow.

Because everything is made by hand, small irregularities in colour and weave are part of each piece's character.
